## Sustained magic, wards and spirits / 持続呪文・結界・精霊

**English**
- Apply continuous spells to targets, accumulate resistance successes to remove them, and receive turn-start reminders.
- Track ley/void-line position and force, with magic and resistance modifiers, including fixed-dice NPCs.
- Resolve wards crossed by spells and Shatter Ward results.
- Link spirit powers to NPCs and place summoned spirits with their force and disposition. Physical defense includes spirit force.
- Defenders can resolve GM-authored sustained spells without owning the casting card; repeated application is prevented.
- Fix resource class choices A–E so resource forms save correctly.
- Preserve the character sheet scrolling fix from v0.12.1.

- 持続呪文の適用、抵抗成功数の蓄積と解除、手番開始時の通知を追加しました。
- レイライン／ボイドラインの位置・フォースを記録し、魔法判定と呪文抵抗に補正します。固定ダイスのNPCにも対応します。
- 結界を通過する呪文の成功数減少と、結界粉砕の結果を処理します。
