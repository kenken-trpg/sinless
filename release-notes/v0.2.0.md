Adds brand / sector play. Install or update with the manifest URL:
```
https://github.com/kenken-trpg/sinless/releases/latest/download/system.json
```

### New
- **Brand actor**: Muscle / Magic / Media / Espionage / Grid statistics, 19 brand traits (slots grow at market cap 10 and 100), market cap, upkeep and bank calculated from resources and assets, sector heat effects
- **Sector actions**: 12 rollable brand actions (TN 4, Competence auto-success, Media penalty at heat ★★; Racketeering and Do Crime raise heat), 3–8 actions per turn by market cap
- **End sector turn**: banks the net market cap, pays shortfalls from the bank, reports unmet upkeep
- **Members**: drag characters onto the brand; asset tests are rolled by the member with the best etiquette
- **Resource item**: class A–E, tags, damage / influence tracks, brand statistic bonuses

### Fixes
- Tests without a pool no longer roll 0 dice

> Changing `system.json` adds new document types. After updating, return to setup and relaunch your world.
