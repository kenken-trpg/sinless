Characters and advancement

Creating a character

  1. Turn on Character creation mode on the Biography tab. The Character creation panel appears at the top of the Main tab.
  2. Give each category (Heritage, Magic, Attributes, Skills, Resources) a priority from A to E, each letter once. Heritage and Magical Talent in the header must fit their priority.
  3. Spend the budgets: Attribute points, Skill points, Knowledge (2×INT), Etiquette (2×CHA) and Zuzus. The panel shows what is spent of each and lists every rule problem, such as a skill above its creation maximum or a budget overspent. A green check means no problems are left.
  4. Use Attribute point adjustment for heritages that change the attribute points.
  5. Roll Ghost Rating (2d6) and Roll seed money (4d6×100) roll in chat and fill in the result.
  6. Finish creation hides the panel. Unspent points and cash are lost; you can turn creation mode back on later.

Lifestyle

Next to Lifestyle in the header:

  • The calendar button pays one sector turn: a prepaid month first, otherwise the monthly cost in zuzus.
  • The cart button buys months of the current lifestyle in advance; the number beside the lifestyle is the prepaid months.

Members of a brand pay automatically when the brand ends its sector turn.

Kismet

The Kismet Pool in the header holds the dice you add to tests; its button refreshes the pool. Advancement is on the Social tab:

  • Award Kismet (Agonarch): a checklist for the end of the session (survived, mission, paydata, goals…), usually 4–6 Kismet.
  • Spend Kismet: raise a skill or an attribute, or the Zoetic Potential. The dialog shows each cost; a new skill costs 4.
  • Choose boon appears for every 10 Kismet earned: a financial windfall, an old friend, skill mastery or guru, a rare item, or pool resilience (a Kismet die for one pool that is never used up).
  • The Advancement log lists awards, purchases and boons. Undo last entry reverses the latest one and refunds its Kismet.
