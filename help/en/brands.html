Brands

A brand actor is the group's organization: its statistics, money, resources and members.

Members

Drag character actors onto the Members tab. Each member's Ghost Rating is shown; asset tests are rolled by the member with the best matching etiquette.

Main tab

  • Brand Statistics: click a statistic to roll it. Each starts at 1; each player may raise one by 1.
  • Brand Traits: click to take or drop a trait, up to the number of slots.
  • Sector heat (header) and its effect are shown at the top.
  • Finances: market cap, upkeep of resources and assets, and the net. Bank (MC) holds the saved market cap. Exchange: −2 banked MC for 10,000ㄓ, 20,000ㄓ for +1 banked MC.

Sector turns

  1. Click a sector action to roll it. The list shows the statistic and dice; actions marked with a fire icon raise sector heat.
  2. End sector turn banks the net market cap, resets the used actions and starts the next turn. Members pay their lifestyle, and unmet upkeep is reported in chat (each unpaid point deals 1d6 damage to a resource).

Resources and assets

  • Resource: class, market cap and upkeep. The +/− buttons track its damage and influence; the flag marks it as controlled by the brand.
  • Asset: click to roll it with the best member's etiquette.
