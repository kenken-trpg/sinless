Rolling a test

  1. Click what you test with on the sheet: a skill, a pool, a defense or an item.
  2. Check the dialog. The line at the bottom shows the limit, the pool dice left and the dice you will roll.
  3. Press Roll. The result is posted as a chat card.

In conflict and outside it

While the actor takes part in a started combat, the test spends dice from its pool and the dialog uses the dice still left. Outside conflict, a skill test rolls the skill rank (Outside conflict) and spends nothing.

Dialog fields

FieldWhat it does
PoolThe pool the dice come from (Brawn, Finesse, Focus or Resolve).
Test typeSkill (skill + gear is the limit), group skill (−2), untrained (whole pool, 4 successes = 1), no limit (dodge, soak, resist) or outside conflict.
Skill rankThe rank used for the limit, or rolled outside conflict.
SpecializationChecked when the specialization applies (+1); unchecked for other uses of the skill (−1). It is pre-checked when the specialization names the weapon, spell or vehicle.
Gear limit bonusAccuracy and other gear that raises the limit.
Firing modeFor firearms: adds recoil tokens.
Ranged modifiersLight, cover, range, point blank, prone target, two guns, aiming and additional targets. Recoil tokens and the capacity are shown; at capacity the attack misses.
Bonus dice / Penalty dicePenalties remove bonus dice first, then lower the limit. Wound penalties are filled in.
Dice drawn from poolLeave empty to draw the most allowed.
Kismet diceDice from the Kismet pool, added to the roll.
Target NumberThe number a die needs to succeed.
RerollReroll 1s, or 1s and 2s.
Required successesOptional. The card then shows success or failure.
Spend pool diceWhether the dice are taken from the pool.

Spells also ask for the force (the dialog shows the drain and whether it is lethal) and for any ward. Defense tests have no limit and no penalty dice.

The chat card

The card shows the dice, the successes and an Argle Bargle Foofaraw. Its owner can add a Kismet die after the roll. Attacks, spells, decking and vehicle tests add buttons to carry the result on; see Chat card buttons.
