The Grid

Decking is on the Grid tab. Create a cyberdeck and software there, then equip the deck with its plug button.

The deck

  • MCP dice join the Focus pool for decking tests only, are spent first, and refresh each round.
  • Threads limit the software loaded and I/O the software running; use the buttons on each program's row. A Base hacking program is needed to hack, rated at least half the MCP. Any problem is listed under the deck.
  • Without a Nerve rig, decking tests take −2. With one, digital hits on the decker are physical damage.
  • Cohesion: the deck's damage track. Click a box to fill up to it.

Hacking

  • Hack device: needs successes of at least twice the device's hardening; successes can accumulate over tests.
  • Brute force hack and Stealth infiltration take a network access node. The card adds NAN influence and raises the alert; the roller extends the influence and the Agonarch raises the alert from the card.
  • NAN influence: beyond 40 m, extending it takes −2 dice, and −2 more every 20 m. The reset button loses control of the node.
  • Intrusion (a world setting) gives a penalty to every decking test. Alert is the world alert level; the Agonarch can change it here.

Software

Click a program to use it with its skill and rating. Programs that need a network node warn when you have no influence. The card shows the alert it raises; running alert-reducing software (Shadow Protocols) lowers it. Digital attacks let the targets defend from the card.

ICE and daemons

For the Agonarch: on an NPC's Grid tab, check Network entity (ICE / daemon). Only its cohesion track (the stun track) takes damage, its Hardening reduces every attack, and its defense dice double against physical and magic attacks.

  • Alert per grid action: a sensor daemon raises the alert by this much more for grid actions in its range.
  • Shrink dice (RNID): adds a button that shrinks a targeted decker's influence by the meters per success.
