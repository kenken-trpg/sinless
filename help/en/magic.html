Magic

Spells, spirits and rituals are on the Magic tab. A character's Magical Talent (header) and Zoetic Potential set what they can do: drain above the Zoetic Potential is lethal.

Powers

CategoryClick the name
Spell, infusionCasts with Sorcery + focus from the Resolve pool. The dialog asks for the force (up to the learned force) and shows the drain and whether it is lethal.
SpiritSummons with Conjuring + focus against the spirit's force, less the spirit bag. The ghost button invokes a bound spirit for a service: drain equal to its force.
AmplificationOpens the power. Amplifications have no test; their zoetic cost always lowers the Zoetic Potential.

Rituals roll their rank from the chosen pool; a ritual with drain adds the Resist drain button to its card.

Casting

  • Target the defenders first: a spell with Damage lets each target defend from the card, resisting with the spell's Resisted with pool.
  • Ward crossed (force) in the dialog: the force of a ward between you and the target; each point removes one success. A spell with Shatter Ward asks for the Target ward (force) instead and attacks that ward.
  • After casting, press Resist drain on the card: Channeling + fetish, then soak.
  • A spell whose duration is continuous and that is resisted with a pool is placed on its targets from the card. Each affected actor gets a Resist reminder at the start of its turns, and the spell ends when enough successes are accumulated. Continuous spells are listed on the Effects tab.

Spirits

To put a summoned spirit on the scene, make an NPC for it and choose that NPC as Spirit actor in the spirit power. The Agonarch can then place it from the summoning card, at the summoned force.

Ley lines

Set where the character stands with Ley line and its force. On a ley line, channeling, conjuring and sorcery gain twice the force in dice; within 2 m, the force in dice. A void line gives its force as penalty dice, and as bonus dice to resist spells.
