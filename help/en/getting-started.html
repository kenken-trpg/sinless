Getting started

This is an unofficial, fan-made system for playing Sinless on Foundry VTT. It contains no rules text: page numbers such as (p.97) point to the rulebook, which you need to play.

Actor types

  • Character: a player character, with the full sheet and character creation.
  • NPC: opponents and allies. NPCs can use fixed bestiary dice instead of pools, and can be network entities (ICE, daemons) or spirits.
  • Vehicle: drones and vehicles, driven by a rigger.
  • Brand: the team's brand for sector play.

Making a character

A new character starts in creation mode. The creation panel on the sheet shows the priorities (A–E) and the budgets for attribute points, skill points, knowledge, etiquette and zuzus, and points out rule problems. When everything is spent, press Finish creation.

The character sheet

The header holds the Kismet pool, the Zoetic Potential and the lifestyle. Below it are the tabs:

  • Main: attributes, dice pools, defenses, skills and condition tracks.
  • Gear: weapons, armor, gear, implants and the vehicle control rig.
  • Magic: spells and powers, rituals and the ley line the actor stands on.
  • Etiquette & Assets: etiquettes, assets and Kismet advancement.
  • Grid: the cyberdeck, software and decking tests.
  • Effects: Active Effects on the actor and its items, including continuous spells to resist.
  • Biography: notes and background.

NPC sheets have Main, Gear, Grid, Effects and Biography.

Rolling

Click a skill name, a pool name, a defense button, or the name or image of an item. A dialog opens; see Rolling a test. The result is posted as a chat card, whose buttons carry the result on; see Chat card buttons.

Dice pools refresh at the start of each combat round (a world setting), or with Refresh pools on the sheet.

Sample actors

The Sample actors compendium holds combat, magic and decking characters, a training NPC, a vehicle and a brand, with their equipment. The Agonarch can open it in the Compendium tab and drag them into the world to try the system.
