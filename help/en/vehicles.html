Vehicles and rigging

Taking control

  1. The rigger needs an equipped VCR (Grid tab). It gives VCR dice: free bonus dice for vehicle tests while hot-seated, refreshed each round, and a number of Links.
  2. Drop the character on a drone or vehicle sheet to make them its rigger. The rigger's Grid tab lists the vehicles they control.
  3. Choose the control mode: Manual −4, VCR −2, Hot-seat 0. Hot-seating also needs a nerve rig; without the gear the mode falls back and the sheet shows the one in effect.

The player needs ownership of both the rigger and the vehicle to roll its tests.

Tests

  • Maneuver, Overdrive and Ram roll the rigger's skill with the control penalty, the vehicle's damage penalty and inertia.
  • Overdrive adds meters to every movement action this round; a street vehicle gains 1 Inertia per overdrive move (−1 die per point).
  • Ram: the target takes Body + 2×inertia; the ramming vehicle's damage is applied from the card, and ramming clears inertia. Battle Ram doubles the damage dealt and halves the damage taken.
  • Mounted weapons fire with the rigger's Gunnery and the weapon's accuracy. The sheet shows the mounted weapon weight against the limit.
  • Soak rolls the vehicle's soak dice. Only a hot-seated rigger can Dodge, with their own Finesse pool.

Damage

Vehicles have a physical track and an integrity track; every 3 damage boxes give −1 die. A hot-seated rigger takes feedback when the vehicle is damaged.
