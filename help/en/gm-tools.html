Tools for the Agonarch

The Agonarch's tools are in the token controls, visible to the Agonarch only.

Operation (spy icon)

  • Set the run difficulty, the operational heat stars and the alert level in one window. Rising heat is announced to everyone with its effects; at four stars the rounds until the High-Threat Response team arrives are rolled.
  • Discovered raises heat to at least one star.
  • Alert responses: give each security response a threshold, and you are told when the alert reaches it. Alert over 50 sets two stars of heat.
  • End operation: sector heat for the brand, the camera table for each character (Ghost Rating losses applied), paydata and relic caches, and reminders for permanent injury tests. Alert and heat then reset.

Car chase (car icon)

  • Start a chase with the legs and paths for the difficulty and a deck of path cards, one per line. A line starting with + is a "leg completed" card.
  • Drop the players' vehicle and the pursuers on the board, or add the selected or targeted tokens.
  • At the end of each turn, flip the primary path. You can also reveal, discard and change the primary path; every change is announced in chat.
  • While a chase runs, vehicle sheets show the rigger's chase actions. Successful cards have an Apply to the chase button.

World settings

In the system settings: run difficulty, operational heat, alert level, intrusion difficulty, base condition boxes, wound penalties and whether pools refresh each combat round.

NPCs

  • Fixed defense dice (dodge, soak, resist) and a weapon's attack dice roll fixed dice instead of a pool. Leave a field empty to use the pool.
  • Agonarch controlled opponents always roll against 4+.
  • For ICE and daemons, check Network entity (ICE / daemon) on the NPC's Grid tab.
  • To place summoned spirits, choose the NPC as Spirit actor in a spirit power.
